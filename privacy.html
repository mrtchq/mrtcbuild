Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026 · MrTC Builds (“MR.TC AI”), operated by Thierry Charlemagne (“we”, “us”, “our”)

This policy explains what information we collect when you use mrtc.me and our memberships — the Starter Lab, Builder's Circle and Mastermind Track — which provide AI business guides, group coaching and community access (together, the “Services”), and how that information is used.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name and email address when you join a membership or create an account.
  • Payment information: subscriptions are billed by Creem, our merchant of record, and its payment processors. We do not receive or store your full card number; we receive confirmation of payment, your plan, and billing status.
  • Community and content activity: posts, comments and messages you share inside the member community, and your progress through guides and member content.
  • Support communications: emails you send to sup@mrtc.me or other support channels.
  • Technical information: device, browser and usage data (pages visited, approximate location from IP address) collected through standard server logs and essential cookies needed to run the site and keep you signed in.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and manage your membership, guides, coaching sessions and community access.
  • To process subscriptions, renewals and cancellations through our merchant of record.
  • To send service emails — onboarding, receipts, schedule and product updates, and member announcements. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time.
  • To answer support requests and improve the Services.
  • To protect the security of the Services and comply with legal obligations.

3. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers who make the Services work — including Creem (payments and subscriptions), Ghost (membership and content platform), email delivery providers, and hosting providers — and only as needed for them to perform their functions. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and users.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies for sign-in, security and site function. If analytics are used, they are limited to understanding aggregate usage of our own pages. You can block cookies in your browser; the site may not work fully without essential cookies.

5. Data retention and security

We keep account information while your membership is active and for a reasonable period afterward for records, tax and dispute purposes. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may cancel your membership at any time. Contact us at sup@mrtc.me and we will respond within a reasonable time, and within any period required by applicable law.

7. Children

The Services are intended for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date and, where appropriate, announced to members by email.
